01 — About

Designing with purpose.

I am an architecture graduate from the Ethiopian Institute of Architecture, Building Construction and City Development (EiABC), Addis Ababa University.

My approach combines spatial thinking, human-centered design, architectural visualization and visual communication to create spaces that are thoughtful, meaningful and responsive to people.

Alongside architecture, I am interested in graphic design, entrepreneurship and sustainable product design.
